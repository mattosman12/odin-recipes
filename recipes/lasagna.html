Lasagna

Description

This delicious recipe will show you how to cook lasagna in a few easy steps!

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Sausage and ground beef
  • Crushed tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Sugar
  • Cheese
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Brown the beef
  2. Make the meat sauce
  3. Prepare the cheese filling
  4. Layer the lasagna ingredients
  5. Bake the lasagna
